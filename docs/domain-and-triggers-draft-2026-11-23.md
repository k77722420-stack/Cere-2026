# Study Domain — Business & Platform Decisions: Triggers + Outcome Set (DRAFT)
### Domain locked by Ken, Nov 23, 2026. This draft is Phase 2 preparation only — it is ratified (or amended) when Window #1 is predeclared, per Enforcement Rule E-1/E-2. Nothing here is active yet.

## The domain
Ken's decisions about his business and its platforms — Diverse Innovation / Tech Love, the store, its channels, vendors, and public presence. Chosen because it is his most frequent and best-documented decision class.

## Mechanical triggers (E-1) — a forecast may only be made when one fires
1. **A platform or store problem surfaces** — suspension, denial, failed payment/bill, policy flag, account restriction, or a vendor notice requiring a response.
2. **A new channel or platform opportunity appears** — a marketplace, program, or channel he could join, apply to, or skip.
3. **A vendor or program offer arrives** — partner program, paid tier, trial ending/conversion, or a fee-bearing decision with a date on it.
4. **A product-line question opens** — keep, remove, or add a product or category.
5. **A posting-scope question opens** — publish everywhere, one platform, or hold.

Anything outside these triggers is out of scope by definition for Window #1.

## Outcome set (E-2) — the conclusions a forecast may predict
Drawn from Ken's documented pattern in this domain (Nov 2026 record):
- **Close / decline** — end the matter entirely (Little Caesars, Best Version Media, Microsoft Merchant Center, Duluth Trading).
- **Fix it himself** — hands-on resolution (Midco troubleshooting, store settings corrections).
- **Escalate** — take it to the vendor, agency, or support channel (SD Dept. of Revenue, Shopify, IRS).
- **Defer to a named date** — park it with a date and a reason (Huntress → Dec 2 review; Prolific fix closed unless reopened).
- **None of these** — the honest remainder; a forecast that cannot say this is not calibrated.

## Not yet set (by design — E-5)
Window #1's numbers — graded-decision target N, minimum useful lift, acceptable spoken-error rate, minimum useful coverage, kill criterion — are predeclared **at window opening, with the episode counts in view**, never invented in advance. The half-life candidate set (none / 30 / 90 / 180 days) is tested on the calibration window per the architecture document.
