# Study Charter & Enforcement Rules — Predictive Pattern Memory
### Issued Nov 23, 2026, by Ken's direction: **all recommendations enforced** — Perplexity's design verdict (adopted) and every recommendation in Lux's senior audit (A-1…A-9) — with senior DevOps/executive rigor.
### Status: BINDING on any future build of this project. A recommendation in the source documents is no longer advice; carried here, it is a rule. **Amendment rule:** these rules change only by a dated policy decision from Ken, recorded the way Duality records policy decisions — never by editing this file in place mid-study. A change opens a new study window; it never rewrites the current one.

---

## Part 1 — Gates (hard stops; no exceptions, no partial credit)

- **G-1 · Merged-main gate** *(audit A-2)*. No study code is built against anything but Duality's **merged main**. The recall-snapshot refactor (311) and salvage (315) land by verdict + Ken's word first. A prototype on an unmerged branch is not evidence about the governed boundary and does not count.
- **G-2 · Data-protection gate** *(audit A-3)*. **No real decision data enters the system** until: (a) an encryption-at-rest decision for study artifacts is recorded; (b) an encrypted backup of study data exists; and (c) a **restore has been executed and verified** — a plan is not a restore. The verdict ledger is classified at the same protection level as the profile, by policy.
- **G-3 · Confirmed-episodes gate** *(audit A-1)*. The Precedent Index accepts **only human-confirmed episodes**. The system may propose an episode from the record; Ken confirms or rejects it; unconfirmed proposals are ineligible as evidence. Episode schema is versioned; episodes are clustered by decision — one decision, one cluster, however many records describe it.
- **G-4 · Shadow-first gate** *(Perplexity §§3, 5; Charter P-2/P-7)*. The system runs in shadow mode — forecasts timestamped, frozen, hidden until Ken commits — until the advancement criteria in E-5 are met. Any visible (pre-decision) mode requires a **separate, explicit opt-in decision by Ken**, recorded as its own experiment with its own evaluation. It is never a settings change.
- **G-5 · Local-data gate** *(Perplexity §3; Charter P-7iii)*. Profile data, episodes, and verdicts go to **no external model or service by default**. Any exception requires Ken's explicit, per-case decision, recorded before the data moves.
- **G-6 · Research-posture gate** *(audit A-8)*. The foundation models (UserLM family, UserRepGen) are research releases. This study is private and single-owner. **Any client-facing or commercial use of any descendant requires a legal review first** — flagged now, while it is cheap.

## Part 2 — Enforced study protocol (rules every run obeys)

- **E-1 · Mechanical decision triggers** *(audit A-4)*. Before a study window opens, the domain's decision points are declared as mechanical triggers (named recurring events), written into the window's charter page. Decisions outside the triggers are out of scope by definition.
- **E-2 · Outcome sets are explicit** *(Perplexity §1)*. Every forecast names its full outcome set in advance, including **"defer"** and **"none of these"** where meaningful. No retro-fitted outcome sets.
- **E-3 · Temporal discipline** *(Perplexity §1)*. Fit on earlier decisions → choose calibration and threshold on later decisions → evaluate the **frozen** policy on a still-later test window. At every forecast, the index, profile, and served recall are reconstructed **as they existed at that decision point**. Future information never leaks backward.
- **E-4 · β = 0 fallback** *(Perplexity §1)*. The model adjuster's blend weight starts at **0** and rises only by the earning procedure in the architecture document (predeclared margin on the calibration window; geometric pool must also beat an arithmetic blend). Uncertain gain → the simpler model stands.
- **E-5 · Predeclared advancement & kill criteria** *(audit A-5; Perplexity §4)*. Before the window opens, the charter page states: the graded-decision target **N**, the **minimum useful lift** over both controls (statistics-only and generic-user), the **acceptable spoken-error rate**, and the **minimum useful coverage**. At N: criteria met → advancement may be *proposed* to Ken; criteria missed → the study **archives with a written negative result**, which is a success of the method. Thresholds are never lowered mid-window to make the system talk.
- **E-6 · Exposure accounting** *(Perplexity §5)*. Every verdict records **exposure status separately from correctness**. Exposed decisions (any forecast seen before deciding) are **excluded from the primary lift analysis**, permanently, for that window.
- **E-7 · Batched reveals** *(audit A-6)*. Post-commit reveals are batched (default: weekly), never per-decision. Reveal cadence is logged as a study parameter, and every result report carries the stated limitation: the primary analysis is *same-decision* uncontaminated, not influence-free across decisions.
- **E-8 · Reporting order** *(Perplexity §1)*. Every report shows, in this order: Brier score and log loss across **all** scored cases → spoken-forecast error **at its coverage** → counts and uncertainty, paired by decision cluster. Accuracy is never reported without coverage beside it.
- **E-9 · Derived-state invalidation** *(Charter P-9; Perplexity §2)*. When a source becomes ineligible or is corrected, all derived artifacts built from it — profile claims, index entries, cached features, pending forecasts — are invalidated and recomputed from currently eligible inputs, or the affected results are suppressed. Lineage is kept at reusable granularity. A profile statement never counts as a vote independent of its episodes (P-3).
- **E-10 · Deletion & the score** *(Charter P-8; Perplexity §2)*. Ken's deletions and corrections are honored immediately in what the profile may assert or reuse. A missed forecast still counts as a miss. Any resulting change in evaluation coverage is **reported in the next scorecard**, never silently absorbed.
- **E-11 · Sealed versioning** *(audit A-7)*. Profile, index, charter, and thresholds are append-only and version-sealed — **profile versions are sealed under the hat** (Ken's geometry, given its honest job: shape and seal, never computation). An in-place edit to any of them invalidates the window's results back to the last sealed version.
- **E-12 · One-tap verdict workflow** *(audit A-9)*. Ken's commit step costs one tap or one short chat message; all structuring is the system's work. The workflow is **piloted for one week with forecasting disabled** before any study window opens; if the commit habit doesn't hold, the workflow is fixed before the predictor is judged.

## Part 3 — Enforcement mechanics

- **Verification, not trust.** A build step counts only when checked against this charter by execution or inspection — the same maker/checker discipline as Duality's rounds (Lux builds, reviewers verify, Ken decides).
- **Violations stop work.** A breached gate or rule halts the affected workstream, is recorded as a dated incident in the project record (stop-record style: what rule, what happened, what was suppressed), and resumes only on Ken's word.
- **The charter travels with the code.** Any repository created for this project carries this charter in its root documents, next to the Prediction Charter (P-1…P-9). A build that cannot point to the rule it satisfies has not satisfied it.
- **Scope.** These rules bind the predictive project only. Duality's core, its contract, and its review stack are unchanged by this charter — which is the wraparound principle, enforced: the wrap earns its place by passing the same doors everything else passes.
